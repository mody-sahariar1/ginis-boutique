Our Story

Good clothes should not be a long journey away.

{{ shop.owner_name }} comes from a village, and she has always loved buying clothes.

She is a schoolteacher by profession, but clothes were always her happy place. That love also showed her a problem that many women around her simply lived with.

For a woman in a village, finding something good is hard work. The better shops are far away. Getting there is not easy. And once you arrive, what you find still has to fit the budget you came with.

Once {{ shop.owner_name }} understood how hard this really was, she finally opened her own shop. {{ shop.name }} is near Golabari Pallimangal School, close to Taki Road, so women nearby can find good clothes on a budget without the long trip.

She keeps it small. She buys only one to three pieces of most designs, and her customers keep in touch through her WhatsApp group. So the piece you choose is one of very few.
